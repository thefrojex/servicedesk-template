# Service Desk (AppFoundry template)

Complaints, engineers, machines and AMC renewals for a copier / printer service business - and a
WhatsApp assistant: the office logs a complaint and assigns an engineer in one message, engineers
close jobs from the site with the part and charge, and the owner asks what's pending, a customer's
history, or which AMCs are due.

- Sign in with your workspace login. The first person to sign in becomes the admin, adds the team
  on the Team page, sets the business name, and links engineers to their logins.
- Starts with sample data for a Delhi NCR service business (15 customers, 4 engineers, ~3 months of
  complaints, AMCs due and lapsed), dated around the day it's loaded. "Reset demo data" in the
  account menu loads it again.
